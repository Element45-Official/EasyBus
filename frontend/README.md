# EasyBus Frontend

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser to walk through the ICT584 frontend. The pages use HTML and CSS, with a local stylesheet that requires no build step to view.

## Passenger flow

1. Open **View Schedules** from the home page to see the [passenger schedule list](schedules.html).
2. Choose **View Seats** on the available sample departure.
3. Pick a seat in the reservation form or open the tracking preview.
4. Use the home page links to preview registration, login, and account settings.

## Admin flow

Open **Admin Portal** from the home page footer to reach [Admin Schedule Management](schedule-list.html), with the add and edit schedule forms, fleet registration, booking management, operations overview, and financial dashboard.

Schedules, bookings, fleet, finances, and tracking display sample data. Form fields show what the servlet will receive. Submit and delete controls are disabled until their servlet routes are connected.
